    ℹ How is this estimated?

    km remaining = (last service mileage + maintenance interval) − current mileage

    Monthly km rate: derived from fill-up mileage records when ≥2 exist (mileage difference ÷ days × 30.4). Falls back to vehicle lifetime average if fewer records exist.

    Months to service = km remaining ÷ monthly km rate

    Estimated cost per service: fleet average of all historical maintenance records — currently ZMW {{ avg_maint_cost|floatformat:0 }}.

    Overdue vehicles (km remaining < 0) are counted immediately in all horizons.
